Five things the court wants you to remember
  1. Who / What / How is your prompt. Write it before the AI writes it for you.
  2. Lie Factor = effect shown ÷ effect in the data. Aim for 1.
  3. Cairo’s five ways charts lie: poor design, dubious data, insufficient data, concealed uncertainty, misleading patterns.
  4. Bars start at zero. Lines fit the meaningful range, and say so. The axis is a decision. Make it on purpose.
  5. You’ll use AI. You still own the chart. Nobody fires the software.
